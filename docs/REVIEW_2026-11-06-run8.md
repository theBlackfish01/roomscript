### Bugs, most severe first

1. **The accusation rig opens on the true answer.** `RoomscriptClosingController.ts:47,101` sets the defaults `suspects[0]`, `zones[0]` and `weapons[0]`, which in the Harlow case are Vale (when you face her), the Study and the cable. `accuse-v3` shows exactly that. Fix: open on "?" (unset) for room and weapon. WHO may default to the suspect you face, which is the intended pointing gesture.
2. **Objection mode sticks on.** `RoomscriptSpatialObjectionController.ts:192` `cancelPull()` never clears `objecting`. A cancelled or tracking-lost pull leaves the next ordinary pull in objection mode. That fires an Object action on a stale claim, costing a pip, or soft-locks the controls.
3. **Dusting collects evidence by accident.** `RoomscriptHandMoments.ts:44-47`: about 1.4 s of hover dispatches PinchObject, costing 10 minutes and cancelling a suspect mid-answer. If the inspect step bails, the prints stay lit and never retry. Fix: dusting reveals the prints; a pinch collects. Guard it while the game is busy or in an accusation.
4. **Presenting evidence leaks a hint.** `Engine/perf/PresentEvidence.ts:13` matches statements from every layer, so presenting the cable or recorder maps to a link that exists only through still-hidden statements. Filter to the suspect's revealed layer, and add a vitest.
5. **Board stamps are wrong.** `RoomscriptCaseBoard.ts:55` stamps CASE CLOSED on wrong and timeout verdicts too. Stamps are never cleared per case (78-82), and each FILED event stacks another one.
6. **Film mode is forced in every Preview run.** `RoomscriptOpeningController.ts:60` sets `filmMode` whenever `isEditor()`, and line 79 hides LEAF overlays every 0.2 s. That hides test failures, which is why the clue failure is "unknown". Gate both to Director runs.
7. **`roomscript-clue` (static read):**
   - The likely failing check is line 42, `SuspectGrid_S1.enabled` after a −60° pitch. `ClueController.ts:106` uses a dot product `> .5`, and cos 60° is exactly 0.5, a float knife-edge.
   - The fixed 350 ms sleeps also race a 0.2 s throttle.
   - Fix: pitch −75° and poll.
8. **Per-frame work** (breaks rule 13):
   - `snapshot()` every frame in `HandMoments:59` (forever if no wall is found) and inside the objection drag (`SpatialObjection:41`);
   - 46 rain objects with new vec3s every frame all session, plus thunder every 14–26 s; native audio blocks for 1+ s (`WindowStorm:38-46`);
   - a subtitle regex and split every frame;
   - palm-dock `setParent` every frame on device.
9. **Material churn and leaks:**
   - `tinted()` and `iconImage()` clone a material on every call;
   - the board and threads rebuild 100+ objects and 30–50 materials on each layout change;
   - a DelayedCallback per case and an UpdateEvent per ending are never removed.
10. **Device placement:**
    - `PRESET_BOARD` and `PRESET_WINDOW` are absolute world positions, so they stay behind when the room is placed elsewhere;
    - props and tape face a hard-coded Preset point;
    - the Dynamic wall search has no fallback;
    - **Accuse exists only on the palm.** If palm-up isn't detected on Specs, there's no way to accuse.
11. **Small issues:**
    - `ClueController.ts:43` undoes the envelope shrink;
    - the seal label stays at "SEAL nn%" after a cancelled hold;
    - `holdAtBeat` isn't reset per case;
    - 512×1024 portraits are drawn into square boxes, so the replay, lightning ghost and board portraits are stretched (`object-replay-v1`).
