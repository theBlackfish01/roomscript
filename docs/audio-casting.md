# Roomscript audio casting — Run 4

Provenance: original fictional voice descriptions by Codex; three previews per role generated by ElevenLabs Voice Design (`eleven_ttv_v3`). No real person was imitated. All previews are retained.

## Selection rubric

This runtime cannot listen to audio: its audio tool reports unsupported audio input. Selection therefore uses an explicit technical rubric, not an invented listening review: zero clipping, adequate speech signal, and speaking pace near the role target. Score = 100 − 0.5 × absolute pace error − 10,000 × clipped fraction − 100 × max(0, 0.25 − active fraction). Ties choose the earlier preview. Voice character comes from the written design brief; tone, accent naturalness, diction and emotional range require Wadood’s listening check tomorrow. Choices are made now so production is not blocked.
