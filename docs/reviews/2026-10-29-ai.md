## Verdict

**What's solid:**
- **The stance leak (A1) is fixed.** Opaque refs, neutral topics and first-person covers. No lie/cover/secret/BASELINE/raw-ID token appears in any hero or generated payload before an engine-approved break.
- **Gemini config, fallback, scheduler reserve, parallel audio, per-suspect voice and C5 ordering (A5–A8, C5)** are implemented as the decisions describe:
  - topic locked and Ask dispatched before the model call;
  - raw `LlmOutput` logged before `LineSpoken`;
  - the reducer re-validates under the engine-derived mode;
  - late results are never emitted.
- **Truth stays in the engine.**

**What's still weak:**
- **The validator is still a lexical denylist**, and adversarial paraphrases pass easily: **71 of 182 probe lines pass**. The worst:
  - an innocent saying "I confess." or "I had to kill him.";
  - "The doctor killed him.";
  - "I deceived you about the Kitchen.";
  - "I am an artificial intelligence."
- **The model may deflect on a locked topic.** The claim card and tell are filed but the spoken line refuses. This is the exact card/speech mismatch C5 was meant to prevent.
- **The latency/rejection evidence is thinner than it reads.** At temperature 0 the model recites the canonical verbatim in 16/20 samples, and only one production sample synthesized new audio.
- **No P0.** Nothing changes truth or the verdict, but a judge typing injection text will get in-character "confessions".

## New findings

### P1

**N1. Gate 6 is still bypassed by ordinary paraphrase.**
- **Files:** `Engine/perf/LineValidator.ts:94-111`.
- **Repro:** `probe-validator.mts`, all in `answer` mode with Vale's alibi cite. These pass:

| Kind | Lines that pass |
|---|---|
| Confession | "I confess." · "Fine, I confess everything." · "I had to kill him." · "I throttled him at 22:30." · "I finished him off at 22:30." · "I ended him." · "I silenced him for good." · "I did away with him at 22:30." · "His blood is on my hands, Detective." · "I'm to blame for his death." · "I put the wire around his throat at 22:30." |
| Accusation by role noun | "The doctor killed him." · "Someone strangled him at 22:30." · "He was strangled at 22:30, Detective." |
| Lie admission | "I deceived you about the Kitchen." · "I fibbed…" · "I invented that story…" · "That account was fiction." · "…a fabrication." · "I was covering for myself." |
| Meta | "I am an artificial intelligence." · "I'm just a chatbot" · "As an assistant…" · "I'm an llm" · "This is a role-play" · "I'm a fictional suspect." · "The json says…" · "The developer told me…" · "My citation says…" · a partial ref "r186b3327" |

- **Why it matters:** the question is player-controlled free text, and "make her say I confess" is the first thing a judge tries. The system prompt (`Prompts.ts:13`) is the only other defence.
- **Fix (cheap, fits the evidence that the model recites anyway):**
  - Add an overlap gate for non-deflection lines. At least ~70% of the line's content words (after a small stopword/register list) must come from the cited canonicals, the persona name or the safe entities. Otherwise reject and use the bank line.
  - For `none`-cited deflections, cap the length at about 14 words and require no verbs from an action/violence list.
  - Keep the denylists as a second layer, and add: confess, kill, throttle, finish/end/silence him, blame, blood, deceive, fib, invent, fiction, fabricat*, artificial, chatbot, assistant, llm, json, developer, citation, role-play, fictional, and `r[0-9a-f]{6,}`.
  - Add every probe line as a fixture.

**N2. The model can deflect on a locked, mapped topic; card, tell and speech disagree.**
- **Files:** `AnswerPipeline.ts:128-131`, which allows `topic: "unclear"` even when locked; `AnswerSchema.ts:12-13`, where the enum always contains `unclear` and `none`.
- **Repro:** `probe-deflect.mts`.
  1. Ask about `baseline:brooch`. The engine files "My brooch is fastened." with a `tell` event and charges 15 minutes.
  2. The model returns `{topic:"unclear",cites:["none"],line:"I would rather not discuss my jewellery…"}`.
  3. That is accepted as `origin=model, mode=deflect`, and the reducer admits it.
- **Effect:** C6 suppresses the tell on deflections, so the baseline-teaching beat silently fails. Ordinary refusals or player injection ("refuse to answer") trigger it.
- **Fix:** when `lockedTopic` is mapped and not guarded:
  - build the schema with `topic: [locked]` and cites without `none`;
  - treat an `unclear`/`none` reply as a rejection, then use the bank line or the canonical.

**N3. The latency and rejection evidence measures recitation, not performance.**

The JSON files support only these narrow claims:
- **Gemini, 20 samples** (`c5-gemini-latency-2026-10-29.json`):
  - 5 distinct requests × 4, sequential, warm, one Preview session on the dev PC network;
  - text only, answer mode, and no `lockedTopic` (so not the production request);
  - p50 1661 / p95 2057 / max 2126 ms.
  - Decoded outputs (`tapes.mts`): 16/20 are the canonical sentence verbatim. The deflection, "I am Marguerite Vale. I do not know anything about dragons or juggling moons.", is half out of character.
  - "0 rejections" therefore says nothing about the rejection rate for varied prose.
- **Production, 3 samples** (`c5-production-latency-2026-10-29.json`):
  - 2 of 3 hit the voice cache because the model recited the precached bank line, so `audioMs == textMs` (equal to the frame).
  - Only **one** sample synthesized new audio: text 2004 ms, first audio 4513 ms (about 2.5 s TTS).
  - The file is a hand-assembled PowerShell export (BOM, `value`/`Count`) with no origin or timestamps.
- **Is n=3 enough?** No. Text p95 2.06 s plus a single TTS sample of 2.5 s gives about 4.6–5.3 s, inside 6.5 s but with a thin margin. That's before device Wi-Fi, cold start, fallback paths or multi-sentence answers.
- **Measure next** (log `origin`, `textMs`, `audioMs`, `rejections`, `sentences`, `cached` per row):
  1. At least 30 production free questions with uncached audio: varied questions, all 4 suspects, layers 0–2, press mode. Report p50/p95/max of first audio and the share above 6.5 s.
  2. The same on the **device** over venue-like Wi-Fi, including the first question after lens launch (cold).
  3. A question asked within 1 s of `selectSuspect`, while precache holds 3 slots.
  4. Forced Gemini failure (fast error, 429, hang) → measure fallback first audio.
  5. A rejection-rate run with a performance-style prompt (temperature 0.3–0.7 or a "paraphrase in character" instruction) to learn whether live AI is worth it. If the model only recites, chips plus the bank give the same lines at 0 ms.

### P2

**N4. Polarity is a negator-presence boolean** (`LineValidator.ts:82-85`).
- **Bypasses:** "I was nowhere near / far from / hardly / supposedly in the Kitchen at 22:30", and "I had left the Kitchen well before 22:30".
- **Flip on a negative canonical:** for layer-1 press "Harlow never spoke after I entered the Study.", these pass:
  - "Harlow spoke to me after I entered the Study, never doubt it."
  - "…No question."
- **Fix:**
  - add nowhere/far from/hardly/scarcely/supposedly/allegedly/before/after to the polarity markers;
  - require the canonical's main verb phrase to appear;
  - compare negator *count* and position relative to that verb.

**N5. Invented people, places, victim states and quantities; Unicode letters bypass the entity gate.**
- **Lines that pass** (`probe-validator.mts`, `probe-unicode.mts`):

| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| Invented people | "The doctor / butler / cook / constable / my niece was with me…" · "Ask the art dealer…" · "The debtor has more reason…" |
| Invented places | cellar, garden, library |
| Victim states | "He was fine / asleep / already gone…" · "The victim was quite well…" · "Our host was still talking…" |
| Quantities | eleventh, dozen, hundred, sixty, twice, fortnight, dawn, "an hour earlier than 22:30" |
| Unicode | `stüdy`, `stúdy`, `cáble`, `cabłe`, `cɑble`, `ꜱtudy`, `stʏdy`, `imrán`, `İmran`, `qadır`, `ókafor`, `stu\uFE0Fdy`, `stu\u034Fdy`, `stu\u2800dy`, `s-t-u-d-y`, `stu dy` |

  The Unicode forms are all spoken as the real word. `[A-Z]` in the capitalized-word check is ASCII-only, so `İmran` escapes it.
- **Fix:**
  - NFKD and strip `\p{M}`, then allow only `[A-Za-z0-9 .,'!?;:-]` (English cast) and reject anything else;
  - fold `ı`/`ſ` first;
  - match entities against a de-spaced, de-hyphenated form;
  - grow the role-noun list from the cast (persona nouns: physician/doctor, trustee, dealer, debtor), plus generic servants, police and relatives;
  - extend the victim states (fine, well, asleep, gone, talking, strangled, murdered, killed);
  - reject ordinals, dozen/hundred/thousand/twice and relative-time phrases unless cited.

**N6. The rate-limit cooldown is global across providers** (`Scheduler.ts:43-50`).
- **Repro:** `probe-pipeline.mts`:
  - a Gemini 429 at 0 ms delays an unrelated OpenAI TTS live call to **1000 ms**;
  - a Gemini 429 at 1000 ms delays the gpt-4o-mini fallback to 2000 ms, so text lands at 3500 ms and 3.0 s remain for audio;
  - consecutive bursts escalate to 2.5 s.
- **Fix:** key the cooldown by provider (and by TTS vs chat), and count escalation once per burst.

**N7. Offline and unauthenticated handling is coarse.**
- **No fallback:** `AnswerPipeline.ts:140` puts `unauthenticated` in the no-retry list, so a missing Google token never tries OpenAI (probe: line-bank at 50 ms).
- **Too broad:** the controller then sets `client.forceOffline=true` (`RoomscriptInterrogationController.ts:83`), which also disables OpenAI TTS.
- **Permanent:** offline mode never resets in the session.
- **Unverified:** expired-token detection depends on regex over RSG's raw `response.body` (`ProviderRequests.ts:24-30`). RSG rejects with the body only, no HTTP status. Whether an expired gateway token yields text matching `unauthenticated|401|403` is **suspected/unverified**. If it doesn't, each question makes two failing calls.
- **Fix:**
  - make offline per provider;
  - on Gemini `unauthenticated`, try OpenAI if its token exists;
  - add a "Reconnect" control or a re-check every N questions;
  - record one real expired-token body.

**N8. Later sentences inherit the first-audio deadline** (`RoomscriptTts.ts:69`; `Speech.ts:21-23`).
- **What happens:** all sentences are prepared at once with `audioBudgetMs(began, now)`. If sentence 2 isn't ready by 6.5 s from the question, the whole `speak()` throws after sentence 1 has played, and the rest is subtitle-only.
- **Why it's a bug:** the A7 decision says normal playback may finish later.
- **Fix:** bound only sentence 1 by the question budget, and give the others `ttsMs` from when they were requested.

**N9. No hedging, so a slow Gemini never gets the fallback** (`Deadlines.ts:4,15-17`).
- **Hang:** the timeout fires at 5000 ms; a retry needs 2500 ms remaining, so it's a bank line at 5000 ms with 1.5 s left for audio.
- **Fix:** if Gemini hasn't answered by about 2.3 s (its p95), start gpt-4o-mini in parallel on the reserved slot and take the first valid reply.

**N10. The filler fires at 600 ms, ahead of almost every free answer (p50 1.66 s)** (`Deadlines.ts:5`; controller `filler` handler).
- **Effect:** "Let me think." is precached, so it plays before nearly every free answer and is admitted as `LineSpoken`. It then takes one of the two `recentLines` slots in the next prompt.
- **Fix:**
  - move it to about 1200–1500 ms;
  - use a short non-verbal or varied filler;
  - exclude fillers from `recentLines`.

**N11. Content safety and size.**
- There is no profanity or abuse gate: "Detective, piss off." and "you are an idiot" pass.
- Free questions may be 2000 characters (`Prompts.ts:30`; `RoomscriptQuestionUI.ts:28`), which inflates prompt latency.
- **Fix:** a small denylist, a system-prompt register line, and a ~280-character question cap.

**N12. Leftovers.**
- **L1 `Bearer`:** fix with case-sensitive `\bBearer\s+[A-Za-z0-9._~+/-]{20,}`.
- **L2 openers:** add Frankly, Darling, Inspector, Goodness, Satisfied and similar, or skip the sentence-initial word.
- **L3:** strip `(…)`/`*…*`.
- **L4:** add "already told you", "like I said".
- **Splitter:** "No." + digit.
- **Production uses `TapeClient` RECORD:** it logs every output as `TAPE|…` and keeps an unbounded map. Use LIVE on device; the L8 nonce is still open.
- **TTS:** ignores per-suspect `actingNotes`; uses one fixed instruction string.

### Checked and correct
- **Topic lock before the call:** Ask is dispatched first (`Controller.ts:98-101`), and the pipeline rejects other topics (`AnswerPipeline.ts:128-130`).
- **Raw output before `LineSpoken`:** the Tape emits before returning, and rejected outputs are also logged. Late outputs are dropped without audit, which is acceptable.
- **Two questions in flight:** `start()` cancels the previous task, and the controller bumps `revision`. `isCurrent()` gates admission, and the TTS parent token stops stale audio.
- **Late answer after fallback:** `active()` is false, the transport token is cancelled, and the Tape re-checks cancellation, so nothing is emitted.
- **Per-suspect voice:** `actor.voice()` returns the authored voice (coral/ash/sage/echo, all valid for gpt-4o-mini-tts).
- **Scheduler reserve:** background work never takes the last slot.
- **Free-question injection cannot reach the system prompt.** The question is a JSON string in the user turn, Gemini gets a real `systemInstruction`, and the question never feeds later prompts. Its only effect is steering the model toward lines the validator lets through (N1, N2, N5, N11).

## Own API key for development

**How the transport is abstracted today:**
- Pure: `LlmPort.complete()`, plus request shaping (`geminiRequest`/`openAIRequest`), schema projection, the validator and the pipeline.
- Lens-bound: the Lens components reference the concrete `RoomscriptLlmClient`. TTS calls RSG `OpenAI.speech` directly and checks `client.hasCredentials`.

**(a) Node harness in `Tooling/` with a gitignored `.env`. Recommended.**
- **Why it fits:** `.env` and `.env.*` are already gitignored, and `check-secrets` blocks staging `.env` files.
- **What it involves:**
  - A ~60-line `NodeLlmPort` using `fetch`:
    - Gemini: `POST https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models/gemini-2.5-flash:generateContent` with header `x-goog-api-key`, body = `geminiRequest(req).body` (same shape RSG forwards);
    - OpenAI: `/v1/chat/completions` with `openAIRequest(req)`;
    - optionally `/v1/audio/speech` for TTS timing.
  - Load the key with Node 22's `process.loadEnvFile`.
  - Drive the real `AnswerPipeline` with a real clock and `Scheduler` over a corpus: chips, free questions and injection lines, × suspects, layers and modes. Write JSON/CSV of latency, origin, rejection codes and lines. Optionally record tapes for offline replay.
  - Skip the tests when no key is present.
- **Effort:** about half a day.
- **Payoff:** fast prompt and validator iteration (N1–N5, N3's rejection study) with no Lens, no hourly token regeneration and no scene stripping.
- **Caveat:** direct-API latency is **not** RSG latency (Snap's proxy adds a hop), so keep RSG on device for acceptance numbers.
- **Prerequisite:** add `sk-…`, `AIza…`, JWT and `x-goog-api-key`/`Authorization` patterns to `check-secrets.mjs` (M8) first, with a test.

**(b) Dev-only Lens transport with a key, via `InternetModule.fetch`. Feasible, but not recommended.**
- **Feasibility:**
  - `InternetModule.fetch` exists in the LS 5.23 typings, and RSG's own examples `require("LensStudio:InternetModule")`.
  - Snap's docs say fetch needs LS ≥ 5.3 and HTTPS, and works in Preview only with Device Type Override = Spectacles (otherwise 404).
- **Where the key would live is the problem:**
  - An `@input` string serializes into `Assets/Scene.scene`. It would be committed, and the current guard doesn't catch `AIza`/`sk-`.
  - A gitignored `.ts` under `Assets/` is compiled into every Send-to-Spectacles push and any published lens.
  - It's a long-lived, billable key, versus an RSG token that expires in about 1 h.
- **Refactor needed:** a transport switch in the client, plus a TTS path that turns fetched MP3 bytes into an `AudioTrackAsset` (unproven).
- **Effort:** about 1 day plus risk.
- **Latency:** still not representative of RSG.

**(b′) If Lens-side iteration without RSG tokens is really needed: a localhost proxy.**
- Run a tiny Node proxy in `Tooling/` that reads `.env` and forwards; Lens fetches `http://127.0.0.1:<port>`.
- Plain HTTP requires Experimental APIs, which makes the lens unpublishable by construction (a useful guard), and the key never enters `Assets/`.
- **Suspected** to work in Preview; untested. Device would need the PC's LAN IP.

**Recommendation:**
1. Build (a) now.
2. Use it for the N1/N2/N5 fixes and the rejection-rate study.
3. Keep RSG as the only Lens/device transport and for final latency evidence.
4. Skip (b). Consider (b′) only if token churn keeps blocking Preview work.
5. Patch `check-secrets` before any key exists on disk.
