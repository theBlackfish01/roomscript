  - Also, at the start the only unrefuted suggestion is always the envelope (hero and 5/5 generated cases), so the "breathes in" signal is a perfect oracle.

## P1: before or alongside D3/D2

**Game design (engine report N2, N3, N8)**
- **Timeout ends the game with no chance to accuse** (`Clock.ts:6`, `Reducer.ts:77`). The tutorial-style route becomes solvable exactly at minute 180 and is then rejected as `case_closed`. Allow one final accusation at timeout.
- **Questioning is optional and the slowest way to learn.** Questions cost 15 minutes and inspection 10, and objects never lie. The alibi deduction is credited from the lamp alone, even if Vale's alibi was never heard (`HeroCaseHarlow1041.ts:168`). Fix:
  - tie that deduction to the heard alibi;
  - make name and alibi baseline questions free or cheap;
  - consider a 240-minute night.

  The interrogation is the game's selling point; the scoring should reward it.
- **Gaze points at the object that breaks the lie** (N8). This feels guided. Consider pointing at the *topic* object rather than the breaker, or only on later layers.

**Determinism**
- **The refuter order depends on the player's starting pose but isn't logged** (`RoomscriptEngineBridge.ts:18`). Between two poses the revealed card differs in 77 of 100 suggestions, and replays silently diverge. Log the order as a setup action, or fold it into `caseId`. Then check that the visual sweep uses the same pose as the engine (*suspected* mismatch, N9).

**AI (AI report N1–N3)**
- **The validator lets paraphrases through:** 71 of 182 new adversarial lines pass. Examples:
  - an innocent suspect saying "I confess." or "I had to kill him.";
  - "The doctor killed him.";
  - "I deceived you about the Kitchen.";
  - "I am an artificial intelligence."

  Fix: require most of a line's content words to come from the cited canonical claim, and widen the confession, accusation and meta denylists.
- **The validator also rejects natural answers.** "No, Detective. I was in the Kitchen…" fails the polarity check. Measure the false-rejection rate alongside the bypass rate.
- **Deflection on a locked topic** (`AnswerPipeline.ts:128-131`). The model can refuse while the engine files the claim and the tell, and C6 then suppresses the tell, which breaks the baseline teaching beat. Lock the schema's topic enum to the locked topic, and treat a deflection there as a rejection.
- **The latency evidence is thin:**
  - The 20 Gemini samples are 5 requests repeated 4 times, all warm. 16 of the 20 outputs recite the canonical sentence word for word.
  - Of the 3 production samples, 2 hit the voice cache.

  Next measurement: at least 30 uncached production questions with a prompt that actually paraphrases, including cold start, during precache, and with forced fallbacks. Record first-audio time, rejection rate and false-rejection rate.
- **A7:** later sentences inherit the 6.5 s first-audio deadline and get dropped. Give continuation sentences their own deadline.

**Lens (Lens report P1-1 to P1-5)**
- **L1. Wire the shatter.** `shatterAsset` is a private null, and the controller builds its own effect component. It needs an input that points at the instantiated, initially disabled object. Without this change, option (a) or (b) still won't play.

Anchor cards to the object or the crate lid, never between the player and the speaker. Raise card text toward design 03's 0.7–0.9° caption floor.

  Add a "place the case" step and small fixture markers.

## Own API key for local development

**Recommended: a Node harness in `Tooling/` that reads a gitignored `.env`** (about half a day).
- It reuses the engine's fact sheets, `geminiRequest().body`, `openAIRequest`, the answer pipeline and the validator as they are.
- It's good for iterating on prompts and the validator, and for the 30-question rejection and paraphrase study above.
- Its latency won't match Snap's gateway, so device and final numbers still come from the gateway.

**Prerequisites:**
- add `.env` to `.gitignore`;
- add the `sk-`/`AIza` patterns to the secret guard;
- the harness must never write keys into `Assets/`.

**Update (2026-10-29): `Tooling/.env` now holds an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.**
- It's gitignored, and one test call returned HTTP 200 in about 2.9 s.
- Claude isn't available through Snap's gateway, so the game still runs on Gemini, with OpenAI as fallback. Use Claude for things that don't depend on the production model:
  - generating adversarial lines for the validator;
  - judging paraphrase quality and false rejections;
  - drafting content.
- Prompt tuning and latency figures for the shipped game still need Gemini, either through the gateway or with a Gemini key.
- Never read this file from `Assets/`. Add an `sk-ant-` pattern to the secret guard.

**Not recommended: a lens transport that carries the key.**
- `InternetModule.fetch` exists in 5.23, and works in Preview only with Device Type Override set to Spectacles.
- But the key would end up in `Scene.scene` or be bundled into every device push.
- If gateway tokens keep blocking Preview work, a localhost proxy is the safer variant. It's untested; plain HTTP requires Experimental APIs, which makes the lens unpublishable, a useful guard.
